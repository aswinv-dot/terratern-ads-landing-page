Why Germany

A government that built a visa
to bring skilled Indians in.

Germany has over 340,000 unfilled skilled roles and a labour shortage that isn't going away. The Opportunity Card is the government's answer - and your window.

€45K – €90K
Salary range across industries and experience levels
Indian professionals are landing roles in IT, engineering, healthcare, and finance at salaries that reshape their families' financial futures. Stack that with 30 mandatory paid leave days, statutory healthcare from Day 1, permanent residency in 4 years, and free university for your children.
🛂
No job offer needed to enter
Move to Germany without a prior employer. The Opportunity Card gives you up to one year after landing to find and start employment.
🎓
Free university for your children
German public universities charge zero tuition. Relocate with your family and lock in world-class education at no cost.
🗣️
English-first in tech cities
Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, and Frankfurt tech companies run almost entirely in English. You can work, grow, and settle without German in Year 1.

PR in 4 years, EU citizenship follows
Get employed, convert to a work visa, and permanent residency opens at 4 years. EU passport eligibility comes after that.
✈️
26 Schengen countries, visa-free
