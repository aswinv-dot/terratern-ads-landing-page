Why Germany

Europe's Tech Powerhouse.
Built for IT Talent from India.

Germany isn't just a visa destination — it's a career launchpad with strong worker protections, free healthcare, and a booming tech sector actively recruiting Indian professionals.

€45K – €120K

IT Salary Range Across Experience Levels

Entry-level IT roles in Germany start at €45,000/year, scaling to €80,000–€120,000 for senior and specialist profiles. Combine that with 30 mandatory paid leave days, statutory health insurance from Day 1, and strong employment law — the long-term financial value of this move is transformational for Indian IT professionals.

🌍

World's 4th Largest Economy

Home to SAP, Siemens, BMW, Bosch, and Deutsche Telekom — global giants with massive, ongoing IT hiring pipelines across all tech stacks.

🛂

Permanent Residency in 4 Years

Enter on the Opportunity Card, convert to a work visa once employed, and PR eligibility begins at 4 years. EU citizenship follows from there.

🗣️

English-First Startup Ecosystem

Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, and Frankfurt operate almost entirely in English in tech. You can work, network, and grow in Germany without German in your first year.

🎓

Free University for Your Children

Germany's public universities are tuition-free. Bring your family and secure world-class education at zero cost — one of the most powerful benefits for Indian families.

🏥

Statutory Free Healthcare

Employed in Germany? You and your family are covered under public health insurance (GKV) from Day 1 — no out-of-pocket bills for standard care.
