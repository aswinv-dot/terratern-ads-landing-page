Why Germany, Why Now

A country that wrote a visa
specifically to bring you in.

Germany's Opportunity Card was created in 2024 to address 340,000+ unfilled skilled roles. The government wants international talent, and it built a legal pathway to make entry as frictionless as possible.

€45K – €90K
Salary range across experience levels and industries
Indian professionals are landing roles in IT, engineering, healthcare, and finance at salaries that transform the financial trajectory of their families. Add 30 mandatory paid leave days, statutory healthcare from Day 1, and permanent residency after 4 years of employment.
🛂
No job offer needed to enter
Move to Germany without a prior employer. The Opportunity Card gives you up to one year to find work after you land.
🎓
Free university for your children
German public universities charge zero tuition. Relocate with your family and secure world-class education at no cost.
🗣️
English-first tech ecosystem
Berlin, Munich, and Frankfurt tech companies run almost entirely in English. You can work and grow without German in Year 1.
🌍
PR in 4 years
Secure employment, convert to a work visa, and permanent residency opens up in 4 years. EU citizenship pathway follows.
✈️
26 Schengen countries
A German residence permit makes all of Europe accessible. Weekend trips to Paris, Prague, and Amsterdam become routine.
