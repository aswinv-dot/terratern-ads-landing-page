Programme Overview

What is Ausbildung?

Ausbildung is Germany's world-famous vocational training programme, a government-recognised dual system where you work at a company and attend a trade school simultaneously. You earn a salary from day one, with no tuition fees.

🎓
Work + Study Together
4 days at your employer, 1 day at a vocational school. Real experience from day one.
💶
Paid from Day One
You receive a trainee salary throughout the programme. No loans, no debt.
📋
Government Recognised
Official German qualification valid across the EU, opening doors across Europe.
⏱️
2–3 Years Duration
Depending on your field. Healthcare roles typically take 3 years with full employment rights.
🌍
PR Pathway
Complete your Ausbildung and you're eligible to apply for German permanent residency.
🗣️
German Language Support
B2 level is typically required. TerraTern's programme includes language preparation.
